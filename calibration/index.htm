Display calibration

Lay a physical ruler on the glass. Line its zero up with the left red tick, then adjust the span until the right red tick sits exactly on 50 mm. The markings in between follow your adjustment, so check the 10 mm marks along the way too.

1.3.3Straight edge

Runs full-frame on a millimetre grid. Lay a straight edge along the dashed guide and draw one stroke against it. The fit reports two numbers: the residual from a straight line (includes any bow in the edge or glass), and the high-frequency residual after removing a 5 mm moving average, which isolates digitizer noise.

1.3.4Hold still

Rest the tip on a crosshair and hold for 3.5 s. The first 0.3 s is skipped to let contact settle. Reports jitter around the centroid, drift, and the offset between the crosshair and where the digitizer thinks the tip is, which shows parallax across the screen.
